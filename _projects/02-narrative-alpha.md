## Research question

Can time-stamped public investment narratives be converted into falsifiable,
auditable research signals without introducing look-ahead bias?

## Implemented

- Source, retrieval, discovery, and observation-time controls.
- Versioned extractor configurations and input digests.
- Conservative migrations that invalidate stale claims and derived signals.
- Creator-balanced signal construction and strictly subsequent market-data
  alignment.
- A deterministic synthetic demo, strict typing, CI, and 120 automated tests.

This project provides research utilities and software validation. It does not
claim validated alpha, profitable trading, or live investment performance.
